## Dancer Onboarding Flow — 12 Steps

The dancer onboarding flow is a guided checkout experience that assembles a personalized welcome package. Steps dynamically appear based on the selected tier.

| Step | Label | Description | Applies To |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Select Your Tier** | Choose your starting dancer tier. This determines your welcome package contents, revenue split, and platform features. | All tiers |
| 2 | **Equipment Plan** | Choose whether CherryStim provides your gear (64/36 split), you lease it (64/36), or you own your own (55/45). | All tiers |
| 3 | **CherryStim Card** | Select your metal debit card tier. All cards build credit via Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. Direct deposit from earnings. | All tiers |
| 4 | **Shipping Address** | Enter your shipping address for welcome package delivery. Worldwide shipping via tracked premium courier. | All tiers |
| 5 | **Platform Heel Size** | Select your shoe size for CherryStim x Pleaser platform heels. Sizes 5–12 available. | Pro, Elite, Premier, Virtuoso |
| 6 | **Fragrance Preference** | Choose your CherryStim fragrance. "Cherry Noir" by Galimard (Grasse) is the signature scent. Additional options from MonLuxe Paris and Ivaré Luxe. | Elite, Premier, Virtuoso |
| 7 | **Lingerie Size** | Select your size for the French lace lingerie collection (Calais Lace, France). 3-piece set: bodysuit, robe, garter. Sizes XS–XXL. | Elite, Premier, Virtuoso |
| 8 | **Swimwear Size** | Select your size for CherryStim premium Italian swimwear (Prato, Italy). 2-piece reversible bikini set. Sizes XS–XXL. | Premier, Virtuoso |
| 9 | **Silk Robe Size** | Select your size for the CherryStim silk satin lounge robe with monogram. Sizes XS–XXL. | Premier, Virtuoso |
| 10 | **Dance Pole Setup** | Confirm your ceiling height for the Lupit LED dance pole. Fits 7'6" to 9'1". Includes installation guide and cleaning kit. | Pro, Elite, Premier, Virtuoso |

## Order Tracking Pipeline — 11 Stages

Once a welcome package order is placed, it enters the CherryStim fulfillment pipeline. Every order tracks through 11 stages, with individual supplier-level status updates visible to the user in real time.

| Stage | Label | Progress | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Order Placed** | 0% | Order confirmed and entered into the fulfillment system. |
| 2 | **Processing** | 10% | Payment verified, shipping address validated, supplier orders initiated. |
| 3 | **Sourcing from Partners** | 20% | Individual items ordered from suppliers worldwide (Galimard in France, Lupit in Slovenia, Pleaser in LA, etc.). |
| 4 | **Suppliers Shipping** | 35% | Partner shipments en route to CherryStim fulfillment warehouse. Individual supplier tracking numbers available. |
| 5 | **Arrived at Warehouse** | 50% | All items received at the fulfillment center. Inventory check against order manifest. |
| 6 | **Assembling Your Package** | 65% | Items placed into the premium matte black gift box with custom-cut foam, cherry-red tissue paper, and gold foil seal. |
| 7 | **Quality Check** | 75% | Final inspection: every item verified against the order, packaging integrity confirmed, personalized welcome letter inserted. |
| 8 | **Shipped to You** | 85% | Package dispatched via tracked premium courier. Master tracking number assigned. |
| 9 | **In Transit** | 90% | Package in the carrier network, moving toward destination. Real-time carrier tracking available. |
| 10 | **Out for Delivery** | 95% | Package on the delivery vehicle, arriving today. Push notification sent to user. |
| 11 | **Delivered!** | 100% | Package received. Confirmation notification sent. Rating prompt for unboxing experience. |
