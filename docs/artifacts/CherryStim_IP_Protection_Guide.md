Protecting CherryStim's intellectual property is essential to maintaining competitive advantage, preventing unauthorized use, and building long-term brand equity in the luxury entertainment technology space.

### Registration Process

**Step 1: Trademark Search**
Before filing, conduct a comprehensive search to ensure each mark is available and not confusingly similar to existing registrations.

- **USPTO TESS Database**: Free search at https://www.uspto.gov
- **Corsearch**: Professional trademark search and brand protection — https://corsearch.com
- **WIPO Global Brand Database**: International search at https://www.wipo.int

**Step 2: File Applications**
File applications through the USPTO Trademark Electronic Application System (TEAS):

- **TEAS Plus** filing fee: $250 per class
- **TEAS Standard** filing fee: $350 per class
- Average time to registration: 8–12 months if no opposition

**Step 3: International Protection**
For global protection, file through the Madrid Protocol via WIPO:

- Designate countries where CherryStim operates or plans to expand
- Priority markets: United States, European Union, United Kingdom, UAE, Japan, South Korea, Australia

## Patent Considerations

### Potentially Patentable Innovations

CherryStim has developed several novel technologies and processes that may qualify for utility patent protection:

**1. Mode-Adaptive Emoji Rendering System**

The CherryStim emoji engine dynamically adapts rendering based on the viewer's active stream mode:

- **2D Mode**: Enhanced depth shadows, float/bounce/explode animations with 50 particles
- **3D Mode**: Full 3D models with physics engine and 100 particles
- **VR Mode**: Interactive catch-and-throw mechanics with spatial positioning and 150 particles
- **Immersive Mode**: All of the above plus haptic pulse triggers through compatible devices with 200 particles

Each of the 25 platform emojis carries mode-specific rendering configurations including 3D model selection, physics simulation parameters, haptic trigger definitions, sound effect assignments, and VR interaction capabilities. The adaptive system is a single engine that detects the active viewing mode and adjusts all rendering parameters in real time.

**Patentability assessment**: Strong candidate. The mode-adaptive approach with automatic parameter switching across four distinct rendering pipelines is novel in the live-streaming space.

**2. Weighted Rating Algorithm with Bad Pattern Detection**

CherryStim's rating system applies asymmetric weighting: client-on-dancer ratings count at 1/4 weight (0.25x), while dancer-on-client ratings count at full weight (1.0x). Combined with the bad pattern detection system (average below 40 across 5+ ratings in 30 days triggers a 2-week rating block), this creates a novel performer-protection mechanism.

**Patentability assessment**: Moderate candidate. The specific combination of asymmetric weighting, threshold-based access restriction (75%), and rolling-window pattern detection may be novel as an integrated system.

**3. Onboarding Checkout Flow with Tier-Gated Package Assembly**

The 12-step dancer onboarding and 8-step client onboarding flows dynamically adjust available options based on the user's tier. Package contents, apparel options, fragrance selections, and equipment configurations all change based on tier level, with real-time cost estimation and multi-supplier order splitting.

**Patentability assessment**: Moderate candidate. The combination of tier-gating, multi-supplier order assembly, and dynamic step filtering in a single checkout flow may qualify as a business method patent.

**4. AR Filter Tier-Gating with Multi-Capability Tracking**

CherryStim's AR filter system gates access to 10 filter categories across 5 client tiers, while each filter independently requires specific combinations of up to 10 tracking capabilities (240-point face mesh, 33-joint body pose, 21-point hand tracking, eye gaze, lip sync, hair segmentation, skin segmentation, and full body segmentation). The system checks both tier access and device capability before enabling a filter.

**Patentability assessment**: Moderate candidate. The dual-gating system (business tier + technical capability) for AR content delivery is a novel approach.

## Trade Secret Protection

### Classified as Trade Secrets

The following CherryStim assets are maintained as trade secrets and are NOT publicly disclosed:

**1. Revenue Split Formulas**

The complete gear-based split system (CherryStim-provided: 64/36, leased: 64/36, dancer-owned: 55/45) and tier-based split system (Rising: 70/30 through Virtuoso: 55/45) with all internal negotiation margins, override capabilities, and promotional split configurations.

**2. Tier Computation Algorithm**

The exact thresholds and weighting formulas used to compute dancer tiers (hours on app, total earnings, gifts received, campaigns completed, modules passed, average rating) and the internal metrics used to adjust these thresholds over time.

**3. Moderation Word Lists and Pattern Library**

The complete list of blocked words (30+), blocked phrases (21+), solicitation regex patterns (7+), leet-speak normalization maps, and variant generation algorithms. While the existence of moderation is public, the exact word lists and detection patterns are trade secrets that would allow bad actors to evade detection if disclosed.

**4. Supplier Pricing and Partnership Terms**

Wholesale pricing from luxury partners (Galimard, Lupit Pole, Pleaser USA, Calais Lace, CARDMAKER, Marqeta), volume discount tiers, exclusivity terms, and co-branding agreements.

### Trade Secret Protection Measures

- **Access controls**: Trade secret information is restricted to authorized employees on a need-to-know basis
- **NDAs**: All employees, contractors, and partners sign non-disclosure agreements before accessing proprietary information
- **Code access**: Source code repositories are access-controlled with role-based permissions
- **Documentation marking**: All internal documents containing trade secrets are marked "CONFIDENTIAL — TRADE SECRET"
- **Exit procedures**: Departing employees must return all materials and acknowledge ongoing confidentiality obligations
- **Monitoring**: Access to trade secret materials is logged and audited

## Brand Guidelines and Usage Rules

### Logo and Wordmark

- The CherryStim logo and wordmark may only be used in accordance with the official brand guidelines
- **Prohibited modifications**: Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, or crop the logo
- **Minimum clear space**: The logo must have clear space equal to the height of the "C" in CherryStim on all sides
- **Minimum size**: The logo must not be displayed smaller than 80px wide in digital or 0.75 inches in print
- **Background requirements**: The logo may only be placed on solid black, solid white, or the official cherry-red (#DC143C) background

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Cherry Red | #DC143C | Primary brand color, accents, CTAs |
| Gold | #FFD700 | Premium tier indicators, luxury accents |
| Matte Black | #1A1A1A | Primary backgrounds, card program |
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | Text on dark backgrounds, clean UI |
| Deep Cherry | #8B0000 | Secondary accents, hover states |

### Typography

- **Primary**: Inter (headings, UI elements)
- **Secondary**: System font stack (body text, long-form content)
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono (developer documentation, code samples)

### Third-Party Usage

Any third party wishing to use CherryStim trademarks, logos, or brand assets must:

1. Submit a written request to the CherryStim brand team
2. Receive explicit written approval before any use
3. Follow all brand guidelines without deviation
4. Include appropriate trademark attribution (™ or ® as applicable)
5. Not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership without a signed agreement

Unauthorized use of CherryStim trademarks will be addressed through cease-and-desist notices, UDRP domain dispute proceedings, or litigation as appropriate.
