Content Policies & Community Guidelines

CherryStim operates as a premium immersive live-streaming platform with 2D, 3D, VR, and fully Immersive streaming modes powered by @react-three/fiber and @react-three/xr. All content and communication must comply with the following community standards.

🌟 Core Principles

  • Zero-Tolerance for Solicitation — Any attempt to exchange money for sexual services is an instant permanent ban. Our platform is entertainment-only.
  • Respect & Consent — All interactions must be consensual. Dancers control their stream, blocking, and muting at all times.
  • No External Payment Solicitation — Requesting CashApp, Venmo, PayPal, or any off-platform payments is prohibited and auto-detected.
  • No Contact Exchange — Sharing phone numbers, addresses, social media handles, or requests to meet in person are blocked.
  • Age-Gated Platform — All users must be 18+ with verified identity. Age verification is enforced at gate entry.
  • Inclusive Environment — Discrimination based on race, gender, sexuality, religion, or disability results in immediate suspension.

🔒 Prohibited Content

  • Sexually explicit / pornographic content
  • Drug use or drug solicitation
  • Violence, threats, or harassment
  • Doxxing or sharing private info
  • Spam, scams, or phishing
  • Intellectual property infringement
  • Impersonation of platform staff

⚖️ Enforcement Tiers

  • ⚠ Warning — First minor offense, message blocked
  • 🔇 Mute — 10-minute mute for repeat violations
  • 🚫 Temp Ban — 24-hour ban after 3+ violations
  • Permanent Ban — Solicitation phrases trigger instant escalation
"CherryStim exists to empower performers with cutting-edge technology in a safe, respectful environment. Every policy exists to protect our community."

⚙️ Moderation Engine Architecture

Processing Pipeline

  • Step 1: Raw input received from chat or voice transcript
  • Step 2: Text normalization (leet-speak → plain text)
  • Step 3: Exact phrase matching against 21 blocked phrases
  • Step 4: Word-level scanning against 34+ blocked words
  • Step 5: Variant matching from pre-computed Set
  • Step 6: Regex pattern matching (7 solicitation patterns)
  • Step 7: Severity classification (none → warning → block → ban)

Enforcement Actions

  • allow: Clean message — no action needed
  • block_message: Message suppressed, user warned
  • mute_user: 10-minute mute for repeat offenders
  • temp_ban: 24-hour ban for serious violations
  • Escalation: 3+ prior violations → temp_ban regardless of severity
  • Phrase match: Always triggers "ban" severity → immediate temp_ban

Rating System Rules & Weighted Scoring

The CherryStim rating system (lib/ratings.ts) uses asymmetric weighted scoring to protect performers from bad-faith client ratings while empowering dancer evaluations of client behavior.

⚖️

Weight Configuration

DirectionWeight
Client → Dancer0.25 (1/4 weight)
Dancer → Client1.0 (full weight)

Client ratings of dancers carry only 25% weight — protecting performers from harassment-driven downvoting.

🎯

Threshold Rules

  • Low Rating Threshold: 75%
  • Low Visibility Threshold: 75%
  • Bad Pattern Block: 14 days
  • Bad Pattern Detection: Avg < 40 over 5+ ratings in 30 days

Data Privacy — GDPR & CCPA

CherryStim is committed to full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU/EEA users and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents. Our data practices align with the strictest global privacy standards.

🇪🇺

GDPR Compliance

  • Lawful Basis: Consent + contractual necessity for platform services
  • Right to Access: Users can export all personal data via dashboard
  • Right to Erasure: Full account deletion with 30-day grace period
  • Right to Portability: Data export in JSON/CSV formats
  • Data Minimization: Collect only what's strictly necessary
  • Breach Notification: 72-hour notification to supervisory authorities
  • DPO Appointed: Data Protection Officer for enterprise oversight
  • Privacy by Design: Built into every feature from architecture
🇺🇸

CCPA Compliance

  • Right to Know: Disclosure of collected categories and purposes
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link
  • Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of privacy choices
  • Financial Incentives: Transparent disclosure of loyalty programs
  • Authorized Agents: Third-party deletion request support
  • Annual Metrics: Published privacy request statistics
  • Under-16 Protection: Opt-in required (N/A — 18+ platform)

Age Verification & Dancer Safety

🔞 Age Verification System

CherryStim is an 18+ age-gated platform. The gate page (app/gate/page.tsx) is the mandatory entry point for all new users. No content is accessible without age verification.

Verification Layers

  • Layer 1: Date of birth confirmation at gate
  • Layer 2: Government ID upload during KYC
  • Layer 3: Biometric age estimation via Jumio
  • Layer 4: Ongoing session verification

Technical Enforcement

  • Middleware checks on all protected routes
  • Supabase role-based access control
  • Session tokens with expiry validation
  • No cached content before verification

💪 Dancer Safety Protocols

Stream Controls

  • Instant Block: One-tap user blocking during live streams
  • Auto-Mute: Moderation engine mutes flagged users automatically
  • Report System: Priority review for dancer-submitted reports
  • Stream Kill Switch: Immediately end stream with one button
  • Viewer Limit Controls: Dancer sets max concurrent viewers

Financial Protection

  • Revenue Splits: Transparent, tier-based compensation
  • Direct Deposit: No intermediaries — earnings go to dancer's card
  • Credit Building: Metro 2 reporting to all 3 bureaus
  • No Chargebacks: Platform absorbs payment disputes
  • Earnings Lock: Confirmed tips cannot be reversed

🤝 Client Conduct Rules

Required Behavior

  • Respectful language at all times
  • Tip through platform only — never external
  • Follow dancer's stream rules & boundaries
  • Report safety concerns immediately
  • Maintain accurate profile information

Zero Tolerance Violations

  • Solicitation of any kind
  • Requesting personal contact info
  • Sending threatening or abusive messages
  • Attempting to circumvent blocks/bans
  • Recording or screenshotting streams
"Our dancers are independent artists — not employees. We provide the technology, safety infrastructure, and financial tools. They control their experience."

📋 Incident Response Protocol

Step 1: Detection — Automated moderation flags or dancer report triggers incident. Real-time alert sent to Trust & Safety team.
Step 2: Assessment — Severity classification (Low / Medium / High / Critical). Stream automatically paused for Critical incidents.
Step 3: Immediate Action — Offending user muted/blocked. Evidence preserved (chat logs, timestamps, user IDs).
Step 4: Investigation — Review chat history, moderation logs, rating patterns. Cross-reference with user's violation history.
Step 5: Resolution — Enforcement action applied (warning → mute → temp ban → permanent ban). Dancer notified of outcome.
Step 6: Follow-Up — Dancer wellness check. Policy review if new evasion pattern detected. Update moderation dictionary if needed.
